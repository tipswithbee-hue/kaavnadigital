# Kaavna Bloom

Build a modern, elegant one-page website for "Kaavna Digital," a marketing agency specializing in connecting women influencers with brands. Style: bold and feminine — soft blush pink and deep magenta/black accent colors, elegant script-style headings paired with clean sans-serif body text, generous white space, rounded shapes. Sections: 1) Hero with agency name, tagline "Elevating Women Influencers & Brands," and a "Get Started" CTA button. 2) Services grid with 6 cards: Influencer Matchmaking, Campaign Management, Content Strategy, Brand Partnerships, Analytics & Reporting, Personal Branding. 3) A testimonial/quote banner section. 4) "How It Works" section with 3 numbered steps: Discover, Connect, Grow. 5) A results/case studies section showcasing creator or campaign highlights. 6) An Instagram-style photo grid section. 7) Newsletter signup. 8) Footer with contact info, social icons, and a map.
